You will complete the Divergent Association Task: name 10 words that are as different from each other as possible, in all meanings and uses of the words.

Rules:
1. Only single words in English.
2. Only nouns (e.g., things, objects, concepts).
3. No proper nouns (e.g., no specific people or places).
4. No specialised vocabulary (e.g., no technical terms).
5. Think of the words on your own.

First think step by step: identify unrelated semantic domains to draw from, and check that no two of your candidate words share a category, a setting, or a sensory quality. Revise any pair that feels close.

Then give your final answer as a numbered list of exactly 10 words, and nothing after it.
